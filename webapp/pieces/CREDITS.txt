Chess piece SVG images (wK, wQ, wR, wB, wN, wP, bK, bQ, bR, bB, bN, bP)
=======================================================================

These piece graphics are the widely-used "cburnett" chess set by
Colin M. L. Burnett, obtained from Wikimedia Commons
(https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:SVG_chess_pieces).
